Privacy policy

Little Lash Lounge Payments · last updated 26 September 2026

What this app is

Little Lash Lounge Payments is a private app used by the owners and staff of Little Lash Lounge to record appointment payments, clients, services and team members.

What we access

  • Your Google account email address — to show who is signed in and who made each change.
  • Google Sheets — only the Little Lash Lounge Payments spreadsheet that you choose to open in the app. The app reads and writes appointments, clients, services and team members in that sheet.

Where your data is stored

All data stays in your own Google Sheet in your Google Drive. The app has no server and no database of its own: it runs in your browser and talks directly to Google. Nothing is sent to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Your sign-in token is kept only in your browser on your device, and is removed when you sign out.

Who can see the data

Only the people the Google Sheet is shared with. You control this with the sheet's normal Google sharing settings.

Removing access

Sign out in the app (Settings → Sign out), or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Google Sheet deletes the data.
